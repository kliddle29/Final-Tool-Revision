# Feedback Form Questions

Create this as a real Google Form (forms.google.com) and have the
tester fill it out immediately after the session, before talking to
them about it. Paste these in as-is or adjust wording.

1. **Short answer.** What do you think this tool is trying to help you do?
2. **Short answer.** What part of the interface felt most clear?
3. **Short answer.** What part felt confusing?
4. **Linear scale, 1-5** (1 = not at all, 5 = very much). How did the
   tool make you feel when it activated?
5. **Short answer.** Would you realistically use something like this?
   Why or why not?
6. **Linear scale, 1-5** (1 = very difficult, 5 = very easy). Overall,
   how easy was this to figure out without instructions?

Once you have real responses, fill in `usability-results.md` in this
folder with a summary -- don't write that file until the test has
actually happened.
